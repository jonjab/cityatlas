China Urban Growth Ratlas

中国城市空间扩展图集 · 1985–2015

Between 1985 and 2015, the People’s Republic of China underwent the largest, fastest physical urbanization process in human history. This ratlas maps the spatial morphology, expansion rings, and quantitative growth trajectories across 47 diverse Chinese cities—from southern coastal manufacturing hubs and ancient Silk Road oases to central megacities and northeast border outposts.
